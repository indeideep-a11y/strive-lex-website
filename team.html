Meet the Strive Lex Team

Experienced attorneys committed to strategic, responsive, and results-driven representation.

Inderdeep Singh Ahluwalia

Founding Partner

Advocate and founding partner of Strive Lex, advising and representing businesses across corporate and commercial law, with a focus on insolvency and bankruptcy under the IBC, commercial litigation, and arbitration. Appears before the Delhi High Court, District Courts, the National Company Law Tribunal, the Debt Recovery Tribunal, and arbitral tribunals.

He takes a holistic approach to law, looking beyond the immediate dispute to the commercial objectives, contractual position, regulatory exposure and long-term risk behind it, so that advice on strategy, negotiation and forum works as a whole. He advises businesses on matters ranging from contracts, compliance and transactions to disputes, insolvency proceedings and resolution through courts, tribunals and arbitration.

Holds an LL.M. in Corporate Banking & Insurance Law from Amity Law School, Noida, and a B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) from Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University. Enrolled with the Bar Council of Delhi; practises across Delhi NCR in English, Hindi, and Punjabi. His approach is built around a simple discipline: understand the client's problem with complete clarity, apply legal strategy with precision, and drive every matter toward resolution.

Mr. Ram Sunder is a retired Assistant Commissioner of Police with more than thirty-nine years of continuous service in the Delhi Police. His career spans the investigation of serious criminal offences in the Economic Offences Wing, cybercrime, and crisis management, including command of multiple police stations and sensitive investigations involving senior government officials and public authorities.

At Strive Lex, Mr. Sunder advises on investigation strategy and police procedure in complex criminal and financial matters. His advice is directed at how an investigation is structured, what an investigating agency requires to sustain a case, how evidence and documentary records are likely to be read, and how a client should conduct themselves at each stage, from the first notice or summons through to the filing of the charge sheet.

He works alongside the firm’s advocates, who remain responsible for all legal advice and for appearances before courts and tribunals.

Akash Chatterjee

Advocate
  • Delhi High Court
  • NCLT
  • NCLAT
  • Supreme Court
  • Competition Law
  • Corporate Advisory

Mr. Akash Chatterjee is a distinguished legal professional with a primary focus on corporate dispute resolution and corporate advisory. His expertise encompasses a broad spectrum of high-stakes legal matters, marked by his exemplary contribution to some of the most significant corporate arbitrations and disputes in India. With a reputation for excellence and a client-centric approach, Akash has represented numerous Fortune 500 companies as operational creditors, financial creditors, liquidators, resolution professionals, and successful resolution applicants. His adept advocacy before the Hon’ble Supreme Court, National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT), and National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) underscore his profound understanding of corporate law and dispute resolution mechanisms.

Akash’s legal acumen extends into the realm of competition law, where he has actively participated in several landmark cases. His involvement in the Google case, which revolved around a penalty of ₹1300 crore, and his representation of the U.P. Glass Manufacturing Syndicate in the AGI Greenpac–HNG merger case, are testaments to his strategic insights and exceptional advocacy skills. His ability to navigate complex legal and regulatory landscapes has positioned him as a sought-after counsel for both private corporations and public entities facing intricate competition challenges.

Beyond the corporate and competition law domains, Akash has also demonstrated a steadfast commitment to public service through his representation of various Ministries and Government Departments, including the Municipal Corporation of Delhi, before the Hon’ble Delhi High Court. His clientele also includes prominent Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) such as NTPC and the Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India (DFCC), reflecting his versatility and comprehensive approach to legal practice. With a career marked by stellar achievements and unwavering dedication, Akash Chatterjee continues to set a benchmark for legal excellence and integrity in the field of corporate law and beyond.

Abhay Pratap Singh

Advocate
  • Civil Litigation
  • District Courts
  • Delhi High Court
  • Supreme Court of India
  • Property & Title Disputes
  • Recovery & Contract Suits

Mr. Abhay Pratap Singh is an Advocate with fourteen years of dedicated practice in civil law. His practice is centred on civil litigation in its entirety, and he is valued for a thorough, detailed command of civil law and procedure.

He conducts civil matters at every level of the judicial hierarchy, from the District Courts to the High Court and the Supreme Court of India. His work covers the wide range of civil disputes, including property, title and possession disputes, partition, specific performance of contracts, declaration, permanent and temporary injunctions, recovery of money including summary suits, landlord and tenant and eviction matters, easements and boundary disputes, succession and probate, compensation and damages, and commercial and contractual disputes before civil courts.

He handles each matter across its full life: pleadings and written statements, interim applications, framing of issues, evidence, arguments, execution of decrees, and appeals, revisions and reviews before higher courts. His approach rests on careful preparation, close attention to procedure and limitation, and a thorough reading of the record, and clients and instructing counsel rely on him for sound, practical guidance in civil disputes of almost every kind.
