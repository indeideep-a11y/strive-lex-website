About Us

Built on Strategy, Integrity, and Advocacy

Strive Lex was founded in 2020 on a simple idea: clients deserve legal counsel that begins with clarity, proceeds with precision, and ends in resolution.

Our Story

A Firm Built Around Our Clients

Strive Lex was founded in 2020 by Inderdeep Singh Ahluwalia, built on the idea that clients, whether individuals or businesses, deserve legal counsel that is direct, strategic, and genuinely invested in their outcome, not just their case.

Founding year: 2020

Today, Strive Lex advises individuals, startups, and established businesses across corporate, commercial, insolvency, and dispute resolution matters, with the same commitment to clear communication and diligent representation on every engagement.

Our chambers are based in New Delhi, where our attorneys appear before the Delhi High Court, District Courts, NCLT, and DRT, with advisory and arbitration work extending to clients across India. Through our International Legal Support practice, we also assist overseas law firms and businesses that need a reliable point of contact in India.

Our Mission

Strive for Justice. Excel in Law.

Our name reflects our philosophy. We strive relentlessly on behalf of our clients and hold ourselves to excellence ("lex") in the practice of law. Every matter, large or small, receives the same rigour and attention.

This philosophy is also what our mark stands for. It reflects our process. We begin by understanding a client's problem with complete clarity and responsibility, taking the time to know exactly what our client needs rather than assuming it. From that clarity, we apply legal strategy with precision. And that precision is what carries a matter to resolution. Clarity. Precision. Resolution. It is not a tagline for us. It is how we work.
