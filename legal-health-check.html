Free Resource

Startup Legal Health Check

Answer ten quick questions and get an honest, general read on where your legal basics stand. Takes about two minutes.

Version: v1.0 Last Reviewed: 27 July 2026 Scope: General guidance for Indian private companies; not jurisdiction-specific
Please note: This tool provides general educational information only, based on your own answers. It does not review your actual documents or facts, does not constitute legal advice, and does not create an advocate-client relationship. For guidance specific to your business, please book a consultation.
1. Is your company properly incorporated, with statutory registrations (PAN, GST if applicable, and ROC filings) up to date?
2. Do all founders have a written founders' agreement covering equity, vesting, and roles?
3. Has all intellectual property created for the business (code, brand, content) been formally assigned to the company?
4. Do you use written contracts for your major customers, vendors, and contractors, rather than verbal or email-only agreements?
5. Does your company track statutory compliance deadlines (ROC filings, tax, labour law) on a calendar?
6. If you have employees or contractors, do you have written employment or consulting agreements in place?
7. Have your key commercial contracts been reviewed by a lawyer in the last 12 months?
8. If you've raised funding or plan to, do you have clean cap table records and shareholder documentation?
9. If you collect any customer or user data, do you have a basic data privacy or confidentiality policy?
10. If a dispute arose with a co-founder, customer, or vendor tomorrow, do you know what your contracts say about resolving it?

This is a general self-assessment based on your own answers, not a review of your actual documents or facts. Book a consultation → for guidance specific to your business.
