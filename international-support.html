India-Facing Legal Support for International Firms

Paralegal, research, and back-office support for overseas law firms, in-house counsel, and businesses that need a reliable India-based extension of their team.

Who This Is For

A Dedicated Point of Contact in India

If your firm or business regularly encounters Indian law questions, needs documents reviewed under Indian contract terms, or requires on-the-ground coordination with Indian courts, regulators, or counsel, Strive Lex can function as your India desk — a single, responsive point of contact who understands both the local legal landscape and the standards international teams expect.

This is separate from our core Indian litigation and advisory practice. It is structured specifically for foreign law firms and businesses that want dependable support without the overhead of hiring or supervising an in-house India team.
