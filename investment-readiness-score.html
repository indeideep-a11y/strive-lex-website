Investment Readiness Score

A general read on how prepared your legal basics are before you start fundraising conversations.

Version: v1.0 Last Reviewed: 27 July 2026 Scope: General guidance for Indian private companies; not jurisdiction-specific
Please note: This tool provides general educational information only, based on your own answers. It does not review your actual documents or facts, does not constitute legal advice, and does not create an advocate-client relationship. For guidance specific to your business, please book a consultation.
1. Is your company properly incorporated with all statutory registrations and ROC filings up to date?
2. Do you have a clean, documented cap table with no undocumented equity promises?
3. Has all IP created for the business been formally assigned to the company, rather than held personally by founders?
4. Do all co-founders have a signed founders’ agreement in place?
5. Are your key customer and vendor relationships governed by written contracts?
6. Does your company maintain basic financial books and records?
7. Are all employees and contractors engaged under written agreements that include IP assignment?
8. Do you track statutory compliance deadlines (ROC, tax, labour filings) systematically?
9. Have any pending legal disputes, notices, or liabilities been identified and addressed?
10. If you've raised a prior round, have your fundraising documents (SHA/SSA/term sheet) been reviewed by counsel?

This is a general self-assessment based on your own answers, not a review of your actual documents or facts. Book a consultation →
