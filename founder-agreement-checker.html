Founder Agreement Checker

Ten quick questions to check whether your founders’ agreement covers what it actually needs to.

Version: v1.0 Last Reviewed: 27 July 2026 Scope: General guidance for Indian private companies; not jurisdiction-specific
Please note: This tool provides general educational information only, based on your own answers. It does not review your actual documents or facts, does not constitute legal advice, and does not create an advocate-client relationship. For guidance specific to your business, please book a consultation.
1. Do you have a signed founders’ agreement at all, rather than a verbal understanding?
2. Does it clearly state each founder’s equity percentage?
3. Does it include a vesting schedule for founder equity (e.g., vesting over several years with a cliff)?
4. Does it address what happens if a founder leaves early (leaver provisions or reverse vesting)?
5. Does it clearly define each founder’s role, responsibilities, and decision-making authority?
6. Does it require each founder to assign their IP contributions to the company?
7. Does it include non-compete or non-solicit terms applicable while a founder is involved?
8. Does it specify how disputes between founders will be resolved (e.g., mediation, buyout mechanism)?
9. Does it address what happens to a founder’s equity on death, incapacity, or voluntary exit?
10. Has it been reviewed by a lawyer, rather than drafted only from a generic template?
